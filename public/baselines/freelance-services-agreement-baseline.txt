FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Freelance Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of April 10, 2026, between
Northstar Digital Inc. ("Client") and Priya Nakamura, an independent contractor ("Contractor"),
for the design and development of Client's marketing website (the "Project").

1. SCOPE OF WORK. Contractor will design and build a marketing website for Client, including
up to five (5) pages, as described in the attached Statement of Work.

2. REVISIONS. Contractor will provide up to two (2) rounds of revisions to the Project at no
additional cost. Further revisions will be billed at Contractor's standard hourly rate.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT. Client shall pay Contractor a total fee of $6,000.00 for the Project.
Payment is due within fifteen (15) days of Contractor's invoice. Invoices unpaid after 30 days
accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Work product created by Contractor specifically for this Project,
excluding any pre-existing tools, code libraries, or design templates Contractor owned before
this engagement, becomes the property of Client upon full payment. Contractor retains
ownership of any pre-existing materials and grants Client a license to use them as part of the
delivered Project.

5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS. Contractor is an independent contractor and not an
employee of Client. Contractor is responsible for all applicable taxes.

6. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days' written notice. Upon
termination, Client shall pay Contractor for all work completed and approved through the
termination date, plus a kill fee of 20% of the remaining contract value.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Contractor agrees to keep confidential all non-public information about
Client's business disclosed during the Project, and shall not disclose such information to any
third party during or after the term of this Agreement.

8. NON-COMPETE. During the term of this Agreement, Contractor shall not accept a directly
competing project from a business Client has identified in writing as a direct competitor.
This restriction ends when the Agreement ends.

9. LIABILITY. Contractor's total liability under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or
otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client under this Agreement.

10. WARRANTY. Contractor warrants that the Project will be free of material defects for sixty
(60) days following final delivery. Contractor will correct any such defects at no additional
cost within this period.

11. INDEPENDENT WORK PRODUCT. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Contractor's ability to
develop general skills, knowledge, and experience gained while performing the Project.

12. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Client's
principal office is located.

By signing below, the parties agree to the terms above.

Client: Northstar Digital Inc.
Contractor: Priya Nakamura
