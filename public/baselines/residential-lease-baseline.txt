RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026, between
Harborview Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Ellis ("Tenant") for the property located at
482 Birchwood Lane, Unit 3B, Millbrook (the "Premises").

1. TERM. This Agreement begins on March 1, 2026 and continues on a month-to-month basis
thereafter. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay $1,850.00 per month, due on the 1st of each month. Rent not received
by the 5th of the month is considered late.

3. LATE FEES. A one-time late fee of $50.00 will be charged for any rent received after the 5th
of the month. No additional daily fees apply.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850.00 prior to move-in. The
deposit may be applied toward unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, or cleaning
costs. Landlord will return any remaining balance within 21 days of move-out, itemizing any
deductions.

5. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs, or to show the
unit only after giving Tenant at least 24 hours' written or verbal notice, except in a genuine
emergency.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Landlord is responsible for maintaining the Premises in a
habitable condition, including structural repairs, plumbing, and electrical systems. Tenant is
responsible for damage caused by Tenant's own negligence and for routine upkeep such as
changing light bulbs and filters.

7. RENEWAL. This Agreement renews on a month-to-month basis unless either party gives 30 days'
written notice of intent to end the tenancy. Any rent increase requires at least 60 days' advance
written notice to Tenant.

8. EARLY TERMINATION. Should Tenant need to terminate this Agreement early, Tenant remains
responsible for rent only through the end of the notice period, prorated to the move-out date,
provided Tenant gives at least 30 days' written notice.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. Any dispute arising under this Agreement may be brought in a court of
competent jurisdiction. Nothing in this Agreement waives either party's right to a jury trial.

10. PETS. Pets are permitted with Landlord's prior written consent and a refundable pet deposit
of up to $300.

11. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not make structural alterations to the Premises without
Landlord's prior written approval, but may make minor, reversible changes such as hanging
pictures.

12. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises
is located.

By signing below, the parties agree to the terms above.

Landlord: Harborview Properties LLC
Tenant: Jordan Ellis
