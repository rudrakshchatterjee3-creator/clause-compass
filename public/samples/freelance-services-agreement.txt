FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Freelance Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of April 10, 2026, between
Northstar Digital Inc. ("Client") and Priya Nakamura, an independent contractor ("Contractor"),
for the design and development of Client's marketing website (the "Project").

1. SCOPE OF WORK. Contractor will design and build a marketing website for Client, including
up to five (5) pages, as described in the attached Statement of Work.

2. REVISIONS. Contractor shall provide unlimited rounds of revisions to the Project at no
additional cost, for as long as Client deems necessary, until Client is fully satisfied.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT. Client shall pay Contractor a total fee of $6,000.00 for the Project.
Payment is due within ninety (90) days of Contractor's invoice. No interest accrues on late
payments.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. All work product created by Contractor under this Agreement,
including any pre-existing tools, code libraries, design templates, or other materials
Contractor owned before this engagement and uses in performing the Project, shall become the
sole and exclusive property of Client upon creation or use.

5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS. Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee
of Client. Contractor is responsible for all applicable taxes.

6. TERMINATION. Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason,
upon written notice to Contractor. Upon termination, Client owes Contractor only for work
Client has explicitly approved in writing prior to the termination date, and no further
compensation, including any kill fee, is owed.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Contractor agrees to keep confidential all non-public information about
Client's business disclosed during the Project, and shall not disclose such information to
any third party during or after the term of this Agreement.

8. NON-COMPETE. During the term of this Agreement and for eighteen (18) months afterward,
Contractor shall not provide design or development services, in any capacity, to any company
that Client identifies as a competitor, anywhere in the world.

9. LIABILITY. Contractor's total liability under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort,
or otherwise, shall not exceed $100.00, regardless of the amount of damages Client may incur.

10. WARRANTY. Contractor warrants that the Project will be free of material defects for thirty
(30) days following final delivery. Contractor will correct any such defects at no additional
cost within this period.

11. INDEPENDENT WORK PRODUCT. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Contractor's ability to
develop general skills, knowledge, and experience gained while performing the Project.

12. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Client's
principal office is located.

By signing below, the parties agree to the terms above.

Client: Northstar Digital Inc.
Contractor: Priya Nakamura
