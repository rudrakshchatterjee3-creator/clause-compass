RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026, between
Harborview Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Ellis ("Tenant") for the property located at
482 Birchwood Lane, Unit 3B, Millbrook (the "Premises").

1. TERM. This Agreement begins on March 1, 2026 and continues on a month-to-month basis
thereafter. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice, except as otherwise
provided in Section 8.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay $1,850.00 per month, due on the 1st of each month. Rent not received
by the 3rd of the month is considered late.

3. LATE FEES. A late fee of $175.00 will be charged for any rent received after the 3rd of the
month, plus an additional $25.00 for each following day the balance remains unpaid, with no
cap on the total late fee that may accrue in a given month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850.00 prior to move-in. The
deposit may be applied toward unpaid rent, damages, or cleaning costs. Landlord will return
any remaining balance within 45 days of move-out, itemizing any deductions.

5. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the Premises at any time, for any reason, without
providing advance notice to Tenant, including for routine inspection or to show the unit to
prospective tenants or buyers.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance to the
Premises during the term of this Agreement, including structural repairs, plumbing, electrical
systems, and any appliances provided by Landlord, regardless of the cause of the damage.

7. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. If Tenant does not provide written notice of intent to vacate at least
60 days before the end of any rental period, this Agreement automatically renews for an
additional 12-month term at a rent increased by 8%.

8. EARLY TERMINATION. Should Tenant terminate this Agreement before completing 12 consecutive
months of tenancy, Tenant shall owe an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent,
due immediately upon vacating.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively
through binding arbitration administered by an arbitrator selected by Landlord. Tenant waives
any right to a jury trial or to participate in a class action against Landlord.

10. PETS. No pets are permitted on the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent.
Unauthorized pets will result in a $500 fine per occurrence and may be grounds for termination.

11. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not paint, install fixtures, or make any alterations to the
Premises without Landlord's prior written approval.

12. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises
is located.

By signing below, the parties agree to the terms above.

Landlord: Harborview Properties LLC
Tenant: Jordan Ellis
